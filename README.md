# PeerConnect

## Problem Statement

Many students possess valuable skills such as programming, design, public speaking, video editing, and content creation but struggle to find peers who want to learn those skills. Similarly, students who want to learn new skills often have difficulty finding suitable mentors within their community. As a result, valuable peer-learning opportunities are frequently missed.

---

## Solution

PeerConnect is a peer-learning and collaboration platform that connects students based on the skills they offer and the skills they want to learn. Users can discover peers, send collaboration requests, and connect through dedicated collaboration spaces. The platform encourages knowledge sharing, networking, and skill development within the student community.

---
